## Ways of working

I like code review that argues with my decisions, not my formatting. When I
disagree with a review comment, I say so in writing with reasons, then commit
to whatever the thread lands on — being overruled by better arguments is fine,
quietly re-litigating is not. I think best by building something rough and
reacting to it; docs come after the second iteration, when I finally know what
I'm building. Async-first by preference, because written reasoning survives
longer than meetings — though I'll happily jump to a call when a thread goes
three rounds without converging.

## What I'm good at

- Tracing data across systems that disagree — ERP-to-Salesforce reconciliation,
  scraper-to-database entity conflicts
- Making flaky third-party integrations behave within honest budgets
- Turning vague product asks into shipped Next.js surfaces fast
