### Problem

After an FPL deadline, `/fantasy/team` could keep showing the previous gameweek's squad for up to an hour.

### Why now

Caught about 75 minutes after the commit that introduced it (9d03a9a), the same night.

### Approach

Root cause: the current gameweek was resolved *inside* a cache keyed on the manager alone. When FPL's `is_current` moved to the next gameweek, the cache key didn't change — so the cached squad didn't either.

The fact the fix is built on: a gameweek's picks are frozen at its deadline. A manager editing their team in the FPL app is editing the *next* gameweek. So the squad is immutable for a week while the scoreboard next to it changes every few minutes, and they can't share one cache.

Fix: three caches at three lifetimes —

- current gameweek: `minutes` (it's what flips at a deadline)
- picks: `hours`, keyed on `(manager, gameweek)` — a new gameweek is a new key, so it can't be served last week's squad however long the entry lives
- entry and history: `minutes` (points and ranks move during matches)

### Explicitly not doing

Caching picks forever, despite them being immutable. FPL fills in `automatic_subs` once, after the gameweek finishes; an hour's lag on that is fine, a month's isn't.

### Resolution

Shipped in 150aba1. The key itself is now the invalidation: there's no expiry to tune and nothing to bust at a deadline.
